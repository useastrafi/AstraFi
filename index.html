About

What AstraFi stands for and how it will be built.

  • Simple by default

    DeFi tools are often crowded and hard to read. AstraFi keeps every screen focused on one job, so you always know what you are doing and why.

  • Built for Robinhood Chain

    The chain is new and its users are used to familiar, accessible products. AstraFi is designed for that audience from the first day.

  • Transparent progress

    Updates are shared openly on X as each phase gets closer. What is planned is labeled as planned, and what is live is labeled as live.

  • Community first

    Early supporters help shape the direction. Feedback is read, discussed, and used to decide what gets built next.

Roadmap

Four planned phases. Each one builds on the last, and details are shared on X as they are finalized.

  1. Phase 1

  2. Phase 2
